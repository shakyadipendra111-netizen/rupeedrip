Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

RupeeDrip ("we", "us") is a small rewards website. This page explains what information we collect and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address and password. Your password is stored in a protected (hashed) form by our login provider, and we cannot read it.
  • Activity: your points balance, claim times and withdrawal requests.
  • Payout details: the UPI ID you enter when you request a withdrawal.
  • Technical data: IP address and device information, used by our captcha and hosting providers to stop bots and abuse.

How we use it

  • To create your account and show your balance.
  • To review and pay withdrawal requests.
  • To detect fake accounts, bots, VPN abuse and other fraud.
  • To contact you about your account or a payout, if needed.

Who else handles your data

  • Supabase stores accounts and balances.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website.
  • hCaptcha checks that you are a real person when you log in or sign up.
  • Advertising partners may be added in future. They may use cookies or similar technology to show ads. If we add them, we will update this page.

We do not sell your personal information.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is active and for as long as needed to handle payouts, disputes and fraud checks. You can ask us to delete your account and data (see Contact below). We may keep payout records where required by law.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete your data, or to close your account, by contacting us.

Children

RupeeDrip is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
